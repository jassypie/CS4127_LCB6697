Overview

Brunei Darusaalam is on the island of Borneo, located on the Northern seaside part, which shares the island with Sarawak and Sabah states of Malaysia and Kalimantan of Indonesian provinces, the capital of this country is called Bandar Seri Begawan,and it has a total of 4 Districts which are called Brunei-Muara, Tutong, Belait and Temburong. The name Brunei Darussalam means "Brunei, the Abode of Peace".

The mother tongue language of this country is Bahasa melayu. Since foreigners like Chinese and Indians are staying here in Brunei, so both Chinese and Indian dialetcs are also spoken by some locals of Brunei.Brunei follows islam as it's official religion, approximately 67% of the population is Muslim,Brunei has a population of about 390,000, and the name of the Sultan of Brunei is His Majesty Sultan Haji Hassanal Bolkiah Mu'izzaddin Waddaulah.Brunei is known for its beautiful mosques, rich Malay culture, peaceful environment and tropical rainforests.
